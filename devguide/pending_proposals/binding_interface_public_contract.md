# Binding-interface characterization: first design for owner review

## What

The maintainer requests a report on #112, a first response/design draft, review
and improvement of that draft, and publication in the owning issue. They then
specify the same detailed repository-document/public-post route used for #111.
The [first design](../binding_interface_design.md) is the maintained proposal;
the issue post links its exact published source for review by the other owners.

The working result `BindingInterface` composes a binding question, participants/
grouping, contexts, available owner observations, site/footprint relationships,
applicability and interpretation. This is a proposed scientific result, not a
new canonical topographic registry, accepted class or implementation commission.

## How

Review the three TopoMT, PeptideMT and PharmacophoreMT contributions and the
audited BindingSite design/work plan. Keep canonical association/realization
ownership in TopoMT, molecular facts/analyses in MolSysMT, pharmacophoric semantics
in PharmacophoreMT and permitted-information/comparison policy in the consumer.
The document records exact review baselines, conceptual fields, identity and
immutable revision, source/local structure-state maps and operation-specific
applicability, ordinary API sketches and symbolic binary/composite/derived cases.

The review improves the chat draft by making declaration/association/observation
identity explicit, retaining original claim associations, clarifying changes and
check/use lifetime, scoping information roles per consuming application, and
preserving native repeated/alternative pharmacophoric evidence. It adds safe
persistence/quantity boundaries, a contribution-coverage matrix, open owner
decisions and a candidate saved original PISA receiving route.

All examples are authored design material. There is no new measured quantity,
API-emitted record, native hypothesis or molecular calculation. Missing required
proof, contradiction and unsupported capability do not become successful empty
measurements. No universal receipt, mutable-source observer or scientific quality
score is assumed.

## Why

BindingSite represents a regional hypothesis and can be independent of partner
or coordinates. Interface characterization relates specified participants under
a question/criterion, potentially with multiple sites, observations and structural
realizations. Consumers need that contextual coherence and faithful inspection
without owning competing copied geometry or losing evidence through projections.

The three contributions support this direction while retaining unresolved public
contract, owner handshake and receiving decisions. Source availability, saved
provider records and favorable comments are not completed cross-owner adoption.

## What was refuted

- Requiring a new numerical algorithm to justify a useful domain characterization.
- Treating Interface as a BindingSite subtype or the union of two sites.
- Inferring joint molecular participation from atomic roles, banks or separate
  pairwise observations, or inferring cooperativity from joint geometry.
- Creating an independently editable copy of TopoMT's canonical relation registry.
- Treating live handles, matching indices/counts or content hashes as universal
  molecular-source verification.
- Treating repeated evidence as increased weights, alternatives as simultaneous
  constraints, or same-candidate consistency as independent validation.
- Freezing every future owner profile before useful data-only work or making
  all of #112 a prerequisite for #111.
