## What the milestone would consolidate

- The Vina/Vinardo adapter, explicit local box and selection-derived domain
  provenance, normalized poses/results and declared backend capabilities.
- Inspectable prepared receptor/ligand states, native MolSysMT export/maps and
  preparation assessment within the supported profiles. General molecular
  inspection/manipulation remains with MolSysMT.
- Named scores, fixed-pose evaluations, ranking/scoring history, serial prepared
  problem execution and retained failure outcomes through the existing APIs.
- Saved input/result audits, linked-case recovery and explicit software,
  scientific, installed/public and visible evidence distinctions.
- The existing MolSysViewer addon and its Python/DOM contracts, while preserving
  the deferred live browser observation and the public-provider incompatibility.
- Source-level support contracts, unit handling, molecular ownership and the
  corrected Vina temporary-file lifecycle.

This is a useful source checkpoint, not whole-Core/Extended acceptance or a
binding-affinity claim. The existing [Core map](../CORE_MVP_STATUS.md),
[scope comparison](../CORE_EXTENDED_COMPARISON.md), scientific case records and
original producers retain their measured limits. Missing chemistry/templates,
partial/reference reading, public provider pairing and visible C4/C6 keep their
own owners. 1.0.0 still requires both C0–C6 and E1–E5 under DMT-045.
