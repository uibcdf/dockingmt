# Integrated 5X72 control evidence

## What

The current frozen overview covers the original ROOT-order matrix but does not
present the subsequent occupancy, reciprocal, reference, original-rigid,
distance and displacement controls. #69 describes a valid protein-only subset;
the broader evidence is needed to interpret that subset. Integrate all seven
historical stages without creating another scientific campaign.

The [owning issue](https://github.com/uibcdf/dockingmt/issues/70) declares all
seven archive SHA-256 values, output scope and interpretation before this
new synthesis. Original records, producers, failures and earlier #67/#69/four-input
overview outputs remain unchanged. This is retrospective presentation.

## How

Deliver a dated explanation and a compact reproducible ledger of original
operations, selected recorded outcomes and source/reuse links. Authenticate
the seven compressed archives; retain original source/version fingerprints
separately from the new presentation helper. Use standard-library record
reading only, with exact declared unshipped helper/data inputs and check mode.

Separate original/new searches, same-geometry fixed scoring, reference and
constructed conformers, distance measurements and all prescribed offsets.
Keep ligand, ROOT order, search receptor and score receptor distinct. Reused
poses/evaluations and equivalent sham trajectories do not add independent
complexes. Docking `Vina.energies` observations and fixed `Vina.score` components
are different score modes and must not be mixed.

## Scientific limits

P59's existing occupancy intervention improves first recovery and changes fixed
score preference on unchanged poses. P69's reciprocal intervention improves
returned-set coverage while first/minimum-fixed recovery remains zero.
Reference, rigid-geometry, contact and displacement results further restrict
the explanation. Preserve negative results and generated-H confounds;
proximity/component associations do not establish physical causes, interaction
classes, chemical readiness, optimization or a preparation remedy.

Update the overview/audit's active evidence map and dated interpretation, with
clear links to original methods. The finite controls support neither general
affinity/accuracy estimates nor new automatic ROOT/preparation/ranking policy.

## Scope and acceptance

No molecular operation, correspondence, geometry kernel, search, scoring,
refinement or reranking. Such capabilities remain in their existing owners.
Other components' issues and live C4/C6 remain deferred. No public/runtime API,
package release or installed artifact is qualified.

Deliver the synthesis/ledger, meaningful hash/link/population/reuse controls and
check mode. Keep old reproducers passing, regenerate report indexes and run
applicable quality and shared publication/resource gates after final docs,
including committed export. Preserve historical source-proof limitations and
distinguish old scientific producers from new head CI.
