    "\n",

    "## Verify the declared order before measuring\n",
    "\n",
    "MolSysMT selects non-H atoms from the actual captured partner representation.\n",
    "PDBQT supplies elements/labels, not a complete chemical graph; conversion\n",
    "explicitly discards its format-specific torsion tree. No chemistry is inferred\n",
    "or restored. For this fixed case the selected written indices are 0..8.\n",
    "\n",
    "Every original pose must have verified written order. Its full names/elements\n",
    "must agree with that representation, and the nine selected source IDs, names,\n",
    "elements and group identities must equal the original reference **in order**.\n",
    "These assertions verify an existing declared positional map; they discover no\n",
    "new match or symmetry. A changed/missing identity stops before geometry."
